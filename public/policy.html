§ 01

Information we collect

We collect information needed to operate, secure, and improve VeltoCloud. Depending on the features you use, this may include: That includes:

  • Account details — your name and email address, plus authentication information needed to sign you in. Passwords should be handled by secure hashing or your authentication provider.
  • Account and feature activity — such as account actions, uploads, sharing settings, and use of available features.
  • Device & usage data — basic technical information like browser type and technical information such as browser type, IP address, and service logs where needed for security, troubleshooting, and operation.
  • Anything you choose to share — such as a text, documents, images, credentials, and other content you choose to upload, as well as feedback you send us.

Do not upload information you are not authorized to store. If payment features are introduced, the relevant payment provider's terms and privacy disclosures will apply.

§ 02

How we use it

We use information to provide and maintain VeltoCloud, protect accounts and content, and support features you choose to use. Specifically, we may use it to:

  • Personalize settings and provide requested features.
  • Operate your account and manage your saved content.
  • Enable sharing, retrieval, and organization of your content.
  • Troubleshoot errors, prevent abuse, and protect the service.
  • Send important service, security, or policy updates.
§ 03

Storage & security

We use technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. The specific protections depend on the feature and how it is implemented.

  • Connections are protected in transit using HTTPS/TLS where supported.
  • Authentication secrets should be handled by secure hashing or an authentication provider; the implementation determines what the service can access.
  • Access to data is governed by the service's access controls and operational requirements.
  • No online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your account credentials safe and retain backups of important files.
§ 04

Sharing & third parties

We may process or share information with the following parties when needed to provide the service:

  • Service providers who provide hosting, storage, email delivery, AI analysis, or other infrastructure needed to operate features. Their handling of data is subject to the applicable service terms and privacy practices.
  • Legal authorities when we are legally required to respond to a valid request.
  • You — if you choose to make content public or share it with others through a link or sharing feature.
§ 05

Cookies & analytics

VeltoCloud may use cookies or similar technologies for authentication, session management, security, and preferences. Any analytics or other optional technologies used should be described here according to the tools actually enabled on the site.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may affect sign-in or other features.

§ 06

Your rights & choices

Subject to applicable law and the features available, you may request to:

  • Access or obtain a copy of personal information associated with your account.
  • Correct or update account information.
  • Request account deletion and deletion of associated data, subject to legal, security, and operational retention requirements.
  • Opt out of non-essential communications where an opt-out is available.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on it.

§ 07

AI document analysis

If you request AI analysis of a document, the selected content may be sent to the AI provider used to fulfil that request. Only submit content you are authorized to share, and review any notice shown when using the feature. Processing and retention by the provider depend on that provider's terms and settings.

§ 08

VeltoChat messages

VeltoChat is designed to relay messages without keeping them as persistent chat history. Messages pass through server infrastructure to reach recipients, and technical metadata or operational logs may still be processed or retained as needed. TLS protects messages in transit but does not, by itself, provide end-to-end encryption.
